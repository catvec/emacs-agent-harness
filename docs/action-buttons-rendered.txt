Action buttons, as the Emacs pages render them
=============================================

These are the pages' own buffers, rendered headlessly by the same
functions that draw them on screen (no display was available to
take the docs/media pictures again).

Computers page  (lisp/ui/harness-ui-computers.el)
-------------------------------------------------

 Computers ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Home              c-1         client · connected
 capabilities: none   resources: 0
 [View] [Control] [Unregister]

Its buttons' tooltips
---------------------
  [View]         → view the screen of the computer at point (V)
  [Control]      → take control of the computer at point (c)
  [Unregister]   → unregister the computer at point (u)

A reading is never used outside the policy's freshness bound, a
reading is never invented, and the price rule still decides from
whatever reading is used.  A reused reading is not evidence that a
price is current now: the bound is the guarantee.

Background re-reads after a failure: a burst, then a heartbeat.
  burst 1: in 5 s
  burst 2: in 10 s
  burst 3: in 20 s
  burst 4: in 40 s
  burst 5: in 80 s
  after the burst: one probe every 300 s until a read settles
